The destination

What New Year’s Eve in Trancoso feels like

New Year’s Eve in Trancoso is not only about parties. It is a week of beach mornings, warm evenings, long dinners, village walks, music, encounters and a particular sense of freedom that makes the destination so desired at the end of the year.

The Quadrado becomes more alive, the beaches carry the rhythm of summer and the days tend to unfold slowly between sea, garden, shade, food and conversation.

For many guests, the choice of where to stay defines the whole experience. A house gives the week a different pace: more space, more privacy, more silence after the celebration and a softer return to the day after.

“Some guests come for the celebration. Most return for the atmosphere.”
